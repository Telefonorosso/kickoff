What happens inside the browser?

The page loads Pyodide and the authenticated official amitools 0.8.1 source archive from PyPI (no wheel installation is required). It places your selected files in Pyodide's temporary in-memory filesystem, runs the same romtool patch … 1mb_rom and, when needed, romtool build -t ext operations used by the command-line milestone, then assembles the final 2 MiB byte image. Only Pyodide, the official amitools source archive, and an optional public Extended ROM are fetched over the network; your selected ROM/module bytes are not transmitted.
